01 / About

A full-stack approach to performance

Over the years I've worked with brands across e-commerce, real estate, higher education, SaaS and service businesses, from single-founder stores to full media productions for Fortune 500 clients, and managed USD 4.5M in ad spend across Meta, TikTok, Google and programmatic.

I scale brands through a full-stack performance approach. I don't just manage ad spend; I engineer the entire conversion journey, from the creative that earns attention to the CRM stage where a lead becomes revenue.

Media buying is a commodity. Creative is the variable of success.

So I use data to inform creative strategy, and creative to drive down acquisition costs. Deep technical control of Meta, TikTok and Google Ads, combined with hands-on creative direction, is what turns attention into measurable revenue.

02 / Expertise

Four disciplines, one funnel

Creative, media, measurement and retention are run as one system, because a cheap click is worthless if the page, the follow-up or the tracking lets it go.

Creative Direction & Strategy

Ideas and hooks built on audience pain points and psychological triggers, then tested with the same rigour as media.

  • Creative strategy. Angles, hooks and messaging frameworks derived from audience research.
  • Direct-response design. High-converting UGC video scripts, static ad frameworks and motion graphics.
  • Modular testing. Hooks, bodies and CTAs tested separately to find winning combinations scientifically.
  • Content briefing. Creators and designers directed to produce social-first assets.

Paid Media Management

Full-funnel buying across the platforms where intent and attention actually live.

  • Meta & TikTok. Scaling structures with CBO and ABO, Advantage+ and broad targeting.
  • Google Search & Performance Max. Complex keyword architectures for high-intent demand.
  • LinkedIn ABM. Account-based targeting for B2B lead generation.
  • Programmatic. Eskimi DSP and Oracle BlueKai for reach beyond the walled gardens.
  • Retargeting. Multi-touchpoint sequences that reduce funnel drop-off.

Data, Analytics & Tracking

Measurement that survives privacy changes and tells the truth about top-of-funnel spend.

  • Measurement protocols. Full setup of GA4, Google Tag Manager and the Meta Conversions API.
  • Attribution modelling. Beyond last click, to understand the real impact of awareness spend.
  • Reporting. Dashboards that tie spend to leads, revenue and pipeline stage.

Lifecycle & Retention

The revenue that happens after the click: email, flows and a CRM that closes the loop.

  • Email & flows. Klaviyo campaigns and automated flows for Shopify stores.
  • CRM pipeline. Lead status, qualification and follow-up feeding back into targeting.
  • Conversion rate. Landing page and offer iteration driven by funnel data.

09 / How I work

Four steps from first call to scale

Every engagement runs the same way, whether it is a retained media account, a creative sprint or a full production. You see the numbers at each step.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes on your goals, your current spend and what is or is not working. You leave with a clear view of what I would change first, whether or not we work together.

  2. 02

    Audit and plan

    I go through the ad accounts, the tracking and the funnel behind them, then come back with a plan: what to fix, what to test first and what the budget should look like.

  3. 03

    Build and launch

    Creative direction, campaign structure, conversion tracking and landing pages, built and live inside the first two weeks rather than after a month of decks.

  4. 04

    Scale and report

    Weekly optimisation against cost per lead or ROAS, and reporting that ties spend to pipeline and revenue instead of stopping at clicks.
