What a freelance marketer gets you that an agency does not

You work with the person running the account. There is no account manager relaying questions to a buyer you never meet, and no junior learning on your budget.

Reporting stops at pipeline rather than at clicks. The real-estate account below is reported on closed deal value, because that is the number the client actually cares about.

Scope flexes. Some engagements are a full retained build, some are a fractional performance lead sitting alongside an in-house team, some are a single audit.

What I take on

  • Paid media management

    Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn: structure, budgets, audiences, bidding and the weekly optimisation that keeps cost per result moving in the right direction.

  • Creative direction and production

    Hooks, scripts, statics and video, shot and edited rather than briefed out. Creative supply is the constraint on most accounts, not targeting.

  • Tracking and measurement

    GA4, Google Tag Manager, the Meta Conversions API and server-side events, so the platforms optimise against real conversions instead of guesses.

  • Lifecycle and retention

    Klaviyo flows and campaigns for e-commerce, and CRM stage mapping so leads can be followed to the closing.

  • Fractional performance lead

    Strategy, forecasting and account oversight for teams that have the hands but need someone senior owning the numbers.

Questions I get asked

How much does a freelance digital marketer in Dubai cost?

It depends on scope and on how much is already in place. A single-platform account with existing creative is a different engagement from a build that includes tracking, creative production and lifecycle. The discovery call ends with a scope and a number, and there is no charge for that call.

Do you work with businesses outside Dubai?

Yes. I am based in Dubai and work across the UAE, in Sri Lanka, and remotely with clients elsewhere. Paid media is run from the platforms, so location matters less than overlap in working hours.

Which industries have you worked in?

UAE real estate is the deepest, followed by e-commerce and higher education. The clients page lists the brands, and the case studies show the accounts in full.

Do you take over an existing account or start fresh?

Usually take over. The first step is an audit of the ad accounts, the tracking and the funnel behind them, which normally finds enough to fix before anything is rebuilt.

What does the first month look like?

Audit and plan in week one, build and launch inside the first two weeks, then weekly optimisation and reporting. The process section on the home page sets out all four steps.
