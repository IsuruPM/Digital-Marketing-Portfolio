Questions I get asked

Does TikTok work for real estate in the UAE?

It generated leads on the account shown, at a blended AED 143.73 and far less in the strongest markets. Whether it works for a given brand depends on the offer and on whether creative can be produced at the rate the platform consumes it.

Can we reuse our Meta creative on TikTok?

It can be tested, but it rarely wins. The platform reads produced-for-Meta assets as ads and the cost shows it, which is why creative production is part of the engagement rather than a separate line.

How many creatives does a TikTok account need?

More than feels reasonable, and refreshed continuously. Creative fatigue arrives faster here than anywhere else in the mix.

Do you run TikTok Shop or e-commerce campaigns?

Lead generation is where the account evidence is. E-commerce work to date has been on Meta and Google, shown in those case studies.
