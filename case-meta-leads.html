The brief

Developers and brokerages need a steady flow of enquiries the sales floor can call the same day. The job was volume that did not fall apart as budgets went up, in a market where the same buyers see property ads every day of the week.

What I did

  • Ran lead-form and website-conversion ad sets side by side, so the cheaper mechanic could be found per audience instead of assumed.
  • Built prospecting on both broad targeting and interest stacks, with retargeting sequences behind each.
  • Scaled winners from AED 40 to AED 500 a day, judging each increase on frequency as well as cost per lead.
  • Rotated creative before frequency passed roughly three, which is where cost per lead started to climb in this account.

What it means

The spread between the best and worst ad set is eight times, which is the whole argument for running mechanics against each other rather than picking one. The cheapest ad set delivered 730 leads at AED 6.48, and the account still closed at AED 43.75 per lead after the expensive tests were paid for. Lifetime real-estate spend in the UAE across this and related accounts is around USD 300K.
