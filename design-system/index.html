SOFT GROUND
Design system v1.0 Digital Marketing Portfolio

A quiet, off-white system for marketing work that has to look expensive.

Every value below is measured from the reference layout: a warm grey ground, white cards separated by hairlines rather than shadows, one near-black used for all actions, and five desaturated tints that do the categorising. Copy the tokens, keep the restraint.

05 / Usage

What keeps it from drifting

✓DO
  • Let white cards float on the warm ground — the contrast between them is the whole visual idea.
  • Set display type at 300 and pull tracking to -0.028em. Light and tight, always together.
  • Give every section an uppercase eyebrow. It is the system's structural signal, not decoration.
  • Keep tints at fill-only saturation (L ≈ 92–95%). If a tint reads as a colour, it is too strong.
  • Use full-bleed photography with a 12px radius; let product colour supply all the vibrancy.
✕DON'T
  • Add a brand accent colour. The near-black button is the accent.
  • Bold a heading. Weight 600+ appears only at 13px and below.
  • Stack drop shadows on cards — hairlines carry separation here.
  • Mix radii inside one row, or round a button to anything but a pill.
  • Tighten section padding to fit more in. Cutting the air is what makes it look cheap.
