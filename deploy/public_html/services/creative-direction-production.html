Creative direction and production

Targeting is largely automated now. What is left for a marketer to decide is what the ad says and how it says it in the first three seconds, which is why creative stopped being a deliverable and became the lever.

Book a discovery call See the results

Creative as a system, not a batch

Volume with a structure around it. Ads are produced against named angles, so when one wins you know which idea won rather than which file did.

A permanent test lane. Winners get promoted into scaling campaigns and losers get retired, which keeps the scaling set clean and the learning continuous.

Judged on the right metric. A test ad and a scaling ad do not share a scorecard; holding both to click-through rate is how good ideas get killed early.

What the engagement covers

  • Angle and message strategy

    The named propositions everything else is produced against, drawn from what the account already proved.

  • Hooks and scripts

    Written for the first three seconds, where the cost per result is actually decided.

  • Statics and video

    Shot and edited in house, including on location, rather than briefed out and waited on.

  • Iteration systems

    Variants built so one element changes at a time and the result means something.

  • Creative reporting

    Performance read by angle and format, so the next round starts from evidence.

  • Placement versions

    One idea cut for every placement it has to run in, framed on set rather than rescued in the edit.

Questions I get asked

Do you shoot, or only direct?

Both. Photography and video direction is a service in its own right, and on most paid accounts the production sits inside the engagement.

How many creatives per month?

Set by the spend and the platform. TikTok consumes far more than Meta, and Meta more than search. The plan names a rate rather than a number pulled from the air.

Can you work with our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. Performance creative and brand consistency are not in conflict; most of the tension comes from treating ads as brand films.

Who owns the files?

You do, including the project files, handed over at the end of the engagement.
