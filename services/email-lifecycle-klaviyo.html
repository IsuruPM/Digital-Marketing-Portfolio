Lifecycle & retention

Email, lifecycle and retention

Paid media rents attention. Lifecycle owns it. On most e-commerce accounts the flows quietly out-earn the campaigns while costing nothing per send, which makes neglecting them the most expensive habit in the business.

Book a discovery call See the results

Where the revenue actually sits

Flows beat campaigns on efficiency. They fire on behaviour rather than on a calendar, which is why on the account below they out-earned campaigns on placed orders while campaigns did the wider reaching.

Scale usually breaks revenue per send. A list growing past 144,000 normally dilutes, and holding the per-send number through that growth is the part that takes work.

Deliverability is a revenue question. Sending to people who stopped opening is how a list stops reaching the ones who still do.

What the engagement covers

  • Core flows

    Welcome, cart and browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back and replenishment, written rather than templated.

  • Campaign calendar

    A send rhythm that fits the catalogue and the margin, not a weekly habit.

  • Segmentation

    Engagement tiers, purchase behaviour and predictive values put to use instead of admired.

  • Deliverability

    Sunset policies, list hygiene and authentication so the sends reach the inbox.

  • Reporting

    Revenue per recipient and flow contribution, read against the paid media rather than beside it.

  • List growth

    Pop-ups, forms and incentives that build the list without training everyone to wait for a discount.

Questions I get asked

Do you work with Klaviyo only?

Klaviyo is where the account evidence sits and where Shopify stores are best served. Other platforms are workable; the thinking does not change with the tool.

How long until flows are live?

The core set is normally built and sending inside the first two to three weeks, starting with the flows that earn most.

Can you write the emails as well?

Yes. Copy and design are part of the build rather than something handed back as a brief.

What about SMS?

Where it suits the market and the consent position is clean, it runs alongside email in the same flows.
