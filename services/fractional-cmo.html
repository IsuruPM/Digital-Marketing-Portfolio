Fractional CMO

A fractional CMO is senior marketing leadership without the salary: positioning, channel mix, budget, team and the numbers that go to the board. Where the need is narrower and sits inside paid acquisition, the performance lead engagement is the closer fit.

Book a discovery call See the results

What the role actually covers

Owning the forecast. What the spend should be, what it should return, and what has to be true for that to happen, stated in advance rather than explained afterwards.

Oversight of whoever is executing. In-house, agency or freelance, the work gets reviewed against the plan by someone who has run the accounts themselves.

Decisions, not decks. The output is what to change this week, with the reasoning attached, in a format the founder can act on without a translator.

What the engagement covers

  • Strategy and forecasting

    Channel mix, budget allocation and the forecast the plan is held to.

  • Account oversight

    Regular review of the accounts and the tracking behind them, whoever is running them.

  • Team direction

    Hiring input, briefing standards and the review rhythm that keeps quality steady.

  • Vendor management

    Agency and freelancer selection, scoping and accountability.

  • Board and investor reporting

    The numbers framed so they survive a board meeting.

  • Martech and tooling

    What to buy, what to drop and what to integrate, judged on what the team will actually use.

Questions I get asked

How much time does a fractional CMO take?

Typically a day or two a week, set by the size of the team and the spend. The scope is agreed before it starts rather than billed by surprise.

Do I need a CMO or a performance lead?

A CMO if nobody owns marketing as a whole: positioning, brand, channel mix, team and budget. A performance lead if that is covered and the gap is a senior owner for paid acquisition and its measurement. The two engagements are priced and scoped separately.

Do you take over the accounts?

Only if that is what the engagement calls for. The usual arrangement keeps your team or agency executing while the strategy and oversight sit with me.

Will you help us hire?

Yes: role definition, screening, trial tasks and onboarding.

How is progress reported?

A weekly written review against the forecast, and a monthly session with whoever needs to see it, including the board.
