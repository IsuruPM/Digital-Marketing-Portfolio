TikTok Ads

TikTok ads management

TikTok rewards creative volume more than any other platform and punishes repurposed Meta assets. The accounts that work produce natively, test widely, and move budget to the spread rather than splitting it evenly.

Book a discovery call See the results

What the platform actually rewards

Spread over even splits. On the account below, an AED 290 test bought 83 leads at AED 3.50 in one market while another paid AED 294 for each of its 44. Budget followed that gap instead of sitting on an even split.

Native production, not cropping. A landscape ad resized to vertical reads as an ad, and the cost per result says so. Hooks are written, shot and cut for the platform.

Volume with a read on it. Thirty-five campaigns only helps if the account is structured so the winners stay visible rather than averaging out.

What the engagement covers

  • Lead generation

    Instant forms and landing pages, with lead quality read past the submission.

  • Creative production

    Scripts, hooks, shooting and editing for vertical video, at the rate the platform consumes it.

  • Market testing

    Deliberately wide early tests so the cost spread between markets is visible before the budget commits.

  • Pixel and Events API

    Clean event setup so optimisation has something real to aim at.

  • Spark ads and creator content

    Running creative from real accounts where that beats brand-handle delivery.

  • Budget allocation

    Moving spend toward the markets and ad groups the cost spread actually favours, rather than holding an even split.

Questions I get asked

Does TikTok work for lead generation?

It did on the account shown, at a blended AED 143.73 and far less in the strongest markets. Whether it works for a given brand depends on the offer and on whether creative can be produced at the rate the platform consumes it.

Can we reuse our Meta creative?

It can be tested, but it rarely wins. The platform reads produced-for-Meta assets as ads and the cost shows it, which is why production is part of the engagement.

How many creatives does an account need?

More than feels reasonable, and refreshed continuously. Fatigue arrives faster here than anywhere else in the mix.

Do you run TikTok Shop campaigns?

Lead generation is where the account evidence sits. E-commerce work to date has been on Meta and Google, shown in those cases.
