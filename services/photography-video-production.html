Production

Photography and video production

I shoot as well as buy media, which means the production is planned around what the ad accounts will need rather than delivered as a gallery and reformatted afterwards.

Book a discovery call See the results

Production planned backwards from the ad account

One shoot, many cuts. Framing that survives a 9:16 crop and a 1:1 crop is decided on set, not in the edit, which is where most asset libraries lose half their usable frames.

Shot lists built from angles. The call sheet is derived from the propositions the account needs to test, so the footage maps onto the creative plan.

Event coverage that holds up. Fortune 500 events for Zoom and CNBC, where there is no second take and the deliverable is due the same week.

What the engagement covers

  • Brand and product photography

    Studio and location, shot for catalogue, site and paid social at once.

  • Video direction

    Concept, shot list, direction and edit, for ads and for brand film.

  • Event and conference coverage

    Stills and video, including Fortune 500 event work.

  • Asset libraries

    Delivered in the crops and formats each placement actually takes.

  • Creative direction on set

    Direction of talent and framing against the angles the account is testing.

  • Post-production

    Edit, grade and sound, delivered in the formats each placement takes rather than as one master to cut down later.
