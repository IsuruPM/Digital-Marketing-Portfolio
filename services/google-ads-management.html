Google Ads and PPC management

How the account gets judged

Four snapshots, not one. A single strong month proves nothing, so the account below is reported across four, including the weakest, which is the month spend was pulled back hardest.

The feed before the bid strategy. Most Shopping underperformance is a feed problem wearing a bidding costume: titles, attributes and how the product set is segmented.

Measurement that survives consent. GA4, Tag Manager and server-side tagging, so the conversion data stays usable instead of quietly degrading until the bidding is blind.

What the engagement covers

  • Search campaigns

    Query mining, match type discipline, negative structure and copy written to the intent behind the term rather than the term.

  • Shopping and Performance Max

    Feed quality, product segmentation and asset groups built so PMax can be judged instead of trusted.

  • GA4 and Tag Manager

    Event design, conversion imports and server-side tagging.

  • Landing pages

    Message match and conversion rate work, because the cheapest improvement is usually not in the auction.

    Impression share, and share lost to budget against share lost to rank, read before any bid moves.

Questions I get asked

Do you handle Shopping feeds as well as campaigns?

Yes, and usually first. Titles, attributes and segmentation decide what Shopping can bid on at all, so the feed is where most of the early gain sits.

How do you report on Google Ads?

On return and revenue, with the attribution window stated. Where the business runs on leads rather than transactions, reporting follows the lead into the CRM.

What is your position on Performance Max?

Used where it earns its place and segmented enough to be readable. A PMax campaign that cannot be broken out by asset group and product set is not a campaign, it is a black box.

Can you work alongside our existing agency?

Yes, as a fractional performance lead: strategy, forecasting and oversight while the agency keeps hands on the account.
